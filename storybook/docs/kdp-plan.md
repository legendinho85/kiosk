# Tiffin & Me on Amazon KDP: the plan

*Draft 1, 24 September 2026. Written for the founder of Made Happy.*

Related: [Series bible](series-bible.md), [Print production](print-production.md) and [Compliance checklist](compliance-checklist.md). The last two assume a litho-printed board book with moving parts, which is now the later route. This plan covers the KDP paperback pilot.

> **How to read the evidence.** kdp.amazon.com was blocked during the research, so KDP's rules were read through search extracts and word-for-word copies of KDP Help saved by other projects in August 2026. UK and EU print costs come from four open-source KDP calculators that agree with each other. Anything unconfirmed is marked **(to verify)**. Before setting any price, check KDP's Printing Cost & Royalty Calculator (8.5 × 8.5 in, premium colour, 24 pages).

## 2. Trim size

### Does size affect the price?

- **Print cost: not between picture-book sizes.** KDP has two bands: "regular" (6.12 in wide or less and 9 in tall or less) and "large" (anything bigger). Every picture-book size is large and costs £3.24 at 24–40 pages (to verify).
- **List price: no.** Size only changes KDP's minimum price (£6.48 large, £5.18 regular), both below the £7.99 you should charge anyway.
- **Royalty: a little.** At the same price a regular-size book earns 65p more (£2.80 against £2.15 at £8.99). That's why 6 × 6 in is in the table.

### The candidates

| Trim | Band | UK print cost | Royalty at £8.99 | Look (thumbnail fill) | Handling, ages 2–5 | Spread shape and camera | Min. gutter, % of width |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **8.5 × 8.5 in (216 mm square)** | Large | £3.24 | £2.15 | Reads as a picture book (100%) | Good lap size | 2:1 spread fills ~73% of a sideways phone at ~36 cm; one page fills 62% of an upright frame at ~24 cm | 4.4% |
| 8.25 × 8.25 in (210 mm) | Large | £3.24 | £2.15 | Same, a little smaller (100%) | Same | Same | 4.5% |
| 8 × 10 in portrait | Large | £3.24 | £2.15 | Workbook or early reader (80%) | Fine | Best single-page fit upright (77%); 1.6:1 spread | 4.7% |
| 8.5 × 11 in | Large | £3.24 | £2.15 | Activity or colouring book (77%) | Floppy | Biggest page; 1.55:1 spread | 4.4% |
| 8.25 × 6 in landscape | Large | £3.24 | £2.15 | Small (73%) | Small | Worst: 2.75:1 spread; a page fills 45% | 4.5% |
| 6 × 6 in (custom) | Regular | £2.59 | £2.80 | Mini or board-book look (100%) | Easy, but little to look at | 2:1, like the square | 6.3% |

*Camera figures are geometry estimates for a typical phone lens. Whether custom sizes print in every Amazon store is unconfirmed (to verify).*

### Recommendation: 8.5 × 8.5 in

1. It is the standard KDP picture-book size and the largest square KDP makes (no KDP paperback is wider than 8.5 in), at no extra cost.
2. Ten square covers make the strongest block on the series page and in ads.
3. The 2:1 spread suits a phone held sideways; a square page suits a phone held upright.
4. On a bigger page the curved strip by the fold is a smaller share, leaving more flat area for name spots.

Size barely changes camera tracking itself: once a page fills the frame, the phone sees about the same detail at any size. Flat pages (track each page, not the spread), textured art and consistent printing matter more. The 65p saving of a 6 × 6 in book isn't worth a mini-looking edition with smaller name spots. Trim and ink can't be changed after publishing, so fix them for all 10.

**Knock-on change in the app.** The prototype draws each spread at 1600 × 1000 (16:10). An 8.5 in square spread is 2:1, so scenes must be rebuilt at 2:1 (for example 2000 × 1000, each half one page) or the name won't land on the printed name spot. This is option B in [print-production.md](print-production.md).

## 3. Page plan and design rules

Page 1 is a right-hand page, and the inside covers are always blank (KDP can't print them). So a 24-page book is page 1 alone, 11 spreads (2–3 to 22–23), then page 24 alone.

| Pages | What goes there | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (right) | **Title page:** title, "A Tiffin & Me story", Tiffin waving, "This book belongs to ______" | Glued to the cover hinge: keep everything 1 in (25 mm) from the spine edge |
| 2 (left) | **Copyright and safety page:** © Made Happy 2026, UK postal address, email, ISBN, EU manufacturer line (section 8), reading age | Plain and light |
| 3 (right) | **"Make this book magic":** QR code (25–30 mm), web address in words, three steps for grown-ups, "See the faint stars? That's where your child's name appears", "The story is complete without a phone" | QR at least 1 in from the gutter |
| 4–5 to 18–19 | **8 story spreads:** Book 1's six beats plus two new ones (say, getting ready and the trophy) | Name spots on at least 4 spreads; the lucky pebble on every one |
| 20–21 | **Bedtime spread** ending "Paw in hand, side by side…" | Dusky mid-tones, not flat navy |
| 22–23 | **Activity spread:** "Point to…", "Count the…", find the pebble, find your first letter | Replaces the moving parts |
| 24 (left) | **"Collect all 10":** cover thumbnails, no prices or links | Hinge glue again. KDP may add a blank or printer page after it (to verify): keep the bottom light |
| Back cover | Blurb, series logo, QR code (top or left), web address | Keep the barcode box clear: 2 × 1.2 in, bottom right, 0.25 in from spine and bottom |

About 150–220 words read aloud, set at 18–22 pt. The printed cover must be complete on its own: for example "Goal!" with a separate blank ribbon carrying the faint star, which the app fills with the child's name.

### Design rules

The scene-unit column assumes each spread is drawn as 2000 × 1000 units, where 1000 units = 8.5 in, so about 118 units = 1 in.

| Rule | Measure | Scene units |
|---|---|---|
| Bleed (top, bottom and outer edge only) | 0.125 in (3.2 mm) | 15 |
| KDP minimum from trim for text and key art | 0.375 in (9.6 mm) | 44 |
| Our margin for text and name spots | 0.5 in (12.7 mm) | 59 |
| Faces, text, name spots, QR: distance from the fold | 0.75 in (19 mm) each side; never under 0.5 in | 88 (min 59) |
| Pages 1 and 24 (hinge glue) | Key content 1 in from the spine edge | 118 |
| Across the fold | Background only. No text, thin horizontal lines or name spots | — |
| Name-spot star | Tint at least 15–20%, or it may vanish | — |
| QR code | 25–30 mm incl. 4-module border, level Q, black on white, web address beside it | — |
| Night pages | Textured mid-tones (blue-violet), no big flat navy or black | — |
| Texture | Soft grain and pencil-like edges in every large flat area: hides banding, gives the camera detail | — |
| Tracking | Each page its own target. The QR code tells the app which book it is, so it loads only that book's targets | — |
| Cover | Matte. One background across the 1.4 mm spine; no spine text (allowed only above 79 pages). Series logo and book number on the front | — |

## 4. Print file checklist

**Interior PDF (one per book)**
- [ ] 24 pages, one PDF page per printed page. **Not spreads.** A spread with bleed is 17.25 × 8.75 in; cut it down the middle.
- [ ] Every page 8.625 × 8.75 in (2588 × 2625 px at 300 DPI), with bleed turned on in KDP.
- [ ] Vector art stays vector; grain, blur and other SVG filters rendered at 300 DPI or more on export.
- [ ] Fonts embedded (or outlined). Transparency flattened (a common cause of missing content).
- [ ] One colour space: all sRGB, or all CMYK with one profile (PDF/X-1a preferred). No Adobe RGB or ProPhoto.
- [ ] Large dark areas no heavier than C60 M40 Y40 K100; total ink about 240% or less (common practice; KDP sets no limit). Small text pure black.
- [ ] No blank pages added by us. Under 650 MB. Clean in KDP's online previewer.

**Cover PDF (one per book)**
- [ ] One page: back + spine + front, 0.125 in bleed on every outside edge. Width = 0.125 + 8.5 + spine + 8.5 + 0.125 in; spine = pages × 0.002347 in.

| Pages | Spine | Full cover (width × height) | At 300 DPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | 0.0563 in (1.4 mm) | 17.306 × 8.75 in | 5192 × 2625 px |
| 28 | 0.0657 in | 17.316 × 8.75 in | 5195 × 2625 px |
| 32 | 0.0751 in | 17.325 × 8.75 in | 5198 × 2625 px |

- [ ] Built on KDP's Cover Calculator template (8.5 × 8.5, premium colour, exact page count).
- [ ] Barcode box clear (KDP adds the barcode free; its white box covers whatever is under it). QR code well away from it. No spine text.
- [ ] Fonts embedded, same colour rules, under 40 MB.

**KDP set-up for each title**
- [ ] Premium colour, white paper, 8.5 × 8.5 in, bleed on, matte; free KDP ISBN.
- [ ] "Low-content book" **not** ticked (low-content books can't join a series).
- [ ] AI questions answered (section 8); series name and number; reading age (section 9).
- [ ] Prices set by hand per marketplace; UK shows 0% VAT.

## 5. Pricing and royalties

Royalty = rate × list price − print cost. Since 10 June 2025 the rate is 60% at or above £7.99, $9.99 or €9.99, and 50% below. Amazon sometimes discounts the price shoppers see, but your royalty is still paid on your list price. Printed children's books are zero-rated for UK VAT, so on amazon.co.uk the list price is what the shopper pays. Check that the Rights & Pricing tab shows 0% VAT, because KDP charges 20% on colouring and activity books.

**Recommendation: £8.99 / $10.99 / €10.99.** £8.99 sits just above a standard paperback, which suits a book that also reads the child's name aloud, and is well under half the price of printed personalised books. It earns 39% more per copy than £7.99, which decides whether ads can ever pay (section 7). Price changes are free (up to 72 hours to show): if sales are slow after three or four weeks, drop to £7.99, never lower. As a guide, 100 UK sales at £8.99 earn £215 before ads.

## 6. Series and publishing plan

**Series.** One series name (for example "Tiffin & Me", with no number in it), books numbered 1–10 so each page shows "Book X of 10". Add each paperback as it goes live; the series page takes up to 72 hours to appear. Paperback titles lock at publication, so settle all 10 now.

**ISBNs.** Use KDP's free ISBNs, one per book. The imprint shows as "Independently published" and they can't be used outside KDP. Nielsen ISBNs (£89 for 1, £164 for 10, £369 for 100; one source says £174 for 10, to verify) are only worth it if you add IngramSpark later.

**Title limit.** Reportedly 2 new paperbacks a week from 21 Sep 2026, resetting at 00:00 UTC each Sunday (to verify; KDP Help said 10 a week in August). A title counts when its draft is first saved, and it's unknown whether deleting a draft returns the slot. **Never create test titles.** Check the message on your KDP Bookshelf first; if it still says 10, create all 10 drafts now.

**Proofs.** Order through amazon.co.uk so they print in the UK or EU: up to 5 per order at print cost plus delivery, marked "Not for Resale", about 1–2 weeks to arrive (to verify). Book 1's proof is a **hard gate**: check colour, gutter loss, the faint star, tracking on each page under warm evening light, QR scanning on at least 10 phones, binding strength and any mark KDP adds on the last page. Unsure about the finish? Switch the draft to gloss and order a second proof. For Books 2–10, order a proof on upload but publish once the previewer is clean; fixes go out as updates.

**Week by week** (slot weeks run Sunday to Saturday, UTC; books go live about 3 business days after submission):

| Week | Create drafts | Art and files | Proofs and publishing | Marketing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **20–26 Sep** (now; slots close Sat 23:59 UTC) | #1 Football, #2 Digger | Book 1 rebuilt at 24 pages: 2:1 scenes, flat-page prompts, cover | Check the title limit; confirm print costs in KDP's calculator | Decide titles, series name, author name and price |
| 27 Sep–3 Oct | #3, #4 | Book 1 finished and uploaded; Book 2 art | Order Book 1 proofs | — |
| 4–10 Oct | #5, #6 | Book 2 uploaded; Books 3–4 art | Book 1 proofs arrive: test, then fix the template | Write the description, keywords and A+ design |
| 11–17 Oct | #7, #8 | Books 3–4 uploaded; Books 5–6 art | **Publish Books 1–2** | Series page; Author Central |
| 18–24 Oct | #9, #10 (last slot) | Books 5–6 uploaded; Books 7–8 art | Publish 3–4 | A+ live; free advance copies go out |
| 25–31 Oct | — | Books 7–8 uploaded; Books 9–10 art | Publish 5–6 | Page on your own site; order author copies |
| 1–7 Nov | — | Books 9–10 uploaded | Publish 7–8 | Start a small UK ads test |
| 8–14 Nov | — | — | **Publish 9–10: all 10 live** | — |
| 15–21 Nov | — | Spare week | Fri 20 Nov: last safe date to resubmit anything | — |
| 27 Nov–16 Dec | — | — | — | Black Friday 27 Nov and Cyber Monday 30 Nov. Tell buyers "order by Wed 16 Dec" |

**Christmas cut-offs.** Amazon UK's 2026 dates aren't out. For 2025 they were about 19 December (standard) and 23 December (Prime) (to verify). Print-on-demand adds days at peak, so tell buyers "order by Wednesday 16 December". Christmas Day is a Friday. For your own site, order author copies by about 20 November and check Royal Mail's last posting dates (both to verify).

**Release order:** 1 Footballer, 2 Digger driver, 3 Firefighter, 4 Pilot, then your pick of six from the series bible's careers range (the postie is the strongest gift). Waves help: each pair gets its own new-release boost, the first proof's lessons carry into books 3–10, and early sales show which careers to push.

## 7. Marketing

**On Amazon (free)**
- **Series page and Author Central:** claim all 10 books, one author name on every cover, a series description.
- **Categories:** 3 per book: one subject (sports, construction vehicles, aeroplanes…), one shared (jobs and careers) and one bedtime or toddler category. These are examples; check exact names in KDP's picker and on competitor pages.
- **Keywords:** 7 boxes of up to 50 characters, in buyers' words ("gift for 3 year old", "toddler book about diggers", "stocking filler toddler", "read along picture book"). Don't repeat title words. Never another brand or character (Bizzy Bear, Nosy Crow, Paw Patrol).
- **Description:** honest: "Scan the code to hear your child's name read into the story and see it appear on the page through your phone." Say the name isn't printed. No web addresses, review requests, "free" or time-limited claims.
- **A+ Content:** one design ("Meet Tiffin", "How the magic works", "Collect all 10") applied to all 10 (to verify that one design can cover several books). No QR codes, prices, links or review quotes.

**Ads (optional, not free).** They break even at an ad cost of 24% of sales at £8.99 (19% at £7.99, 27.5% at £9.99). If 1 click in 10 sells, the most you can pay is about 22p a click at £8.99 (16p at £7.99). Book clicks typically cost $0.80–1.30 (generic figures), so ads only pay if buyers go on to buy more of the series. Run a capped UK test from early November (£3–5 a day, one automatic and one keyword campaign across all 10), keep only campaigns under break-even, and raise budgets only 27 Nov–16 Dec.

**Reviews.** Amazon removes reviews from family and close friends. Free advance copies are fine only if no review is required, and reviewers should say the copy was free. UK law (the DMCC Act, since 6 April 2025) bans fake and hidden rewarded reviews, so the app must never unlock anything for a review; "If Tiffin made you smile, a review helps other parents" is fine. Send advance copies to parents who aren't friends: toddler groups, nurseries, parenting groups.

**Gifting.** Gift wrap and messages are unconfirmed for KDP books (to verify at checkout once Book 1 is live). Delivery to another address always works, and page 1's "This book belongs to" line gives givers somewhere to write.

**Own website and author copies.** Author copies are identical to retail copies, cost print plus delivery, come up to 999 at a time once a book is live, and can be resold. Use them for your shop, fairs and local sale-or-return. A 216 mm square book should go as a Royal Mail Large Letter (to verify). The QR code is also a direct line to buyers: an email list is fine if signing up is a separate choice, never a condition of using the app.

**Later: bookshops and hardcover.** Leave KDP Expanded Distribution off: it pays 40% minus print cost (36p at £8.99), raises the minimum price and may exclude ink-heavy full-bleed interiors. The usual next step is Nielsen ISBNs, used on KDP for Amazon and on IngramSpark for shops, libraries and wholesalers such as Gardners. IngramSpark can also make a short hardcover from the same art (to verify).

## 8. Policies and risks

| Area | The rule or risk | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **AI disclosure** | KDP counts Claude-written text and Claude-drawn art as "AI-generated", even after heavy editing. The answer is private to Amazon (to verify). | Answer Yes on every title and republish: text = entire work, images = many AI-generated images. Log the human decisions for each book. |
| **Copyright** | US: purely AI-made material isn't protected (Copyright Office 2025; Supreme Court declined Thaler, 2 Mar 2026). UK: the "computer-generated works" rule is untested and was proposed for repeal on 18 Mar 2026. | Treat the art as easy to copy. The lasting protection is a trade mark, plus records of human creative work. |
| **Trade marks and Bizzy Bear** | A soft, almost-drawn *style* is fine; copying Benji Davies's characters, layouts or cover look is not. KDP bans others' brands in titles and keywords. | Never name Bizzy Bear, Nosy Crow or Benji Davies in listings, keywords or ads, or call our art "Bizzy Bear style". Search UKIPO, then file "TIFFIN & ME", "TIFFIN" and Tiffin's head in classes 16, 9, 41, 28: roughly £170 plus £50 per extra class (to verify). |
| **Titles and metadata** | Title must match the cover and locks at publication; no "free" or "bestselling". | No "{name}" in printed titles; don't say "personalised book" (the print isn't). Settle all 10 now. |
| **Complete book; QR codes and links** | KDP bans sending readers elsewhere for the full content and flags content freely available online. No rule against printed QR codes was found. | The printed story reads fully without a phone (the series bible's print lines). The app stays free: no paywall, email gate or upsell. Keep app pages out of search engines and offer no page downloads (our suggestion). |
| **Moving-part prompts** | books/tiffin-football/book.json says "Lift the flap", "Turn the wheel", "Pull the tab", "Slide", "Push": false promises in a flat book. | Print "Point to…", "Count…", "Find…"; keep flaps, wheels and sliders in the app. |
| **EU product safety (GPSR)** | EU sales need the manufacturer's name, postal address and email. Whether KDP covers this, or a UK publisher needs an EU representative, is unknown (to verify). | Manufacturer block on page 2 of every book; search KDP Help for "GPSR" inside the dashboard before relying on EU sales. |
| **Toy and children's-product law** | A flat paperback is a book, not a toy. A US testing exemption for ordinary books excludes books for children 3 and under. | No CE/UKCA mark or toy warnings. Reading age from 3. Ask a lawyer about the US before promoting on amazon.com. |
| **UK legal deposit** | UK publishers may owe the British Library a copy of each title (to verify). | Check once before launch. |
| **Print quality** | Colour varies between print sites and runs; big dark areas go muddy or streaky; neon greens and blues shift; pale tints vanish; the glued binding isn't toddler-proof; occasional misprints. | Proof first (section 6) and follow section 3's rules. Don't rely on exact brand colours. Watch reviews; KDP support replaces misprints. |
| **Title limit** | The 2-a-week cap is unconfirmed; a wasted draft may cost a week. | Check the Bookshelf message first; no test drafts. |
| **Demand** | One comparable 2026 AI-illustrated 24-page KDP series sold one copy in its lifetime (a single example). | Market alongside production (section 7); the app is the hook. |

## 9. Decisions for you, and next steps

**Decisions (my recommendation first)**
1. **Trim:** 8.5 × 8.5 in, not 6 × 6 in (65p more a copy, but a mini-looking book).
2. **Pages:** 24, or 28/32 at the same print cost but more art across all 10.
3. **Cover finish:** matte, or gloss after a side-by-side proof.
4. **Price:** £8.99 (£7.99 if sales are slow), $10.99, €10.99.
5. **Titles:** a printed pattern without "{name}" (say "Goal!", with the name on a ribbon in the app); the 10 careers and their order.
6. **Series and author name** for every cover (for example "Tiffin & Me" and your name or a pen name).
7. **Reading age:** 3–5 on KDP; marketing can still welcome 2-year-olds. books/index.json currently says 1–4.
8. **Stores:** all Amazon stores from day one, or push the UK first until the EU (GPSR) and US questions are answered.
9. **Ad budget:** a hard cap, for example £5 a day and £150 up to Christmas: the only planned spend beyond proofs.
10. **Trade mark:** search and file in the UK now, or after first sales.

**Next steps**
1. **Today:** read the title-limit message on your KDP Bookshelf; run KDP's calculator (8.5 × 8.5 in, premium colour, 24 pages) for the UK, US and Germany.
2. **By Saturday 26 Sep, 23:59 UTC:** make decisions 1–7 and create paperback drafts #1 and #2 with final titles.
3. **Claude, this week:** rebuild Book 1 as a 24-page square book: 2:1 scenes with page-sized camera targets, flat-page prompts, front matter, activity and "collect all 10" pages, the copyright page with the manufacturer block, print export at 8.625 × 8.75 in per page, and a cover on KDP's template.
4. **Next week:** upload Book 1, clear the previewer, order proofs through amazon.co.uk; create drafts #3–#4 from Sunday 27 Sep.
5. **When the proof arrives:** test it (section 6), fix the template, then run Books 2–10 through the plan.
6. **Tidy the repo:** update titles and ages in books/index.json; mark the board-book sections of print-production.md and compliance-checklist.md as the later route.
